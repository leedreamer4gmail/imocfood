IMOC（International Meat Open-Source Community，国际开源肉制品联盟）是一家专注于高品质牛肉制品的全球化食品公司，核心产品包括薄片牛肉干、和牛脆片、谷饲牛肉干。我们以开源理念重塑肉制品供应链透明度，提供OEM代工、一件代发、出口等全链路服务，总部广州，在南宁、重庆设有现代化生产基地。

IMOC (International Meat Open-Source Community) is a technology-driven food brand specializing in premium Wagyu beef jerky and thin-sliced beef jerky. We provide OEM manufacturing, dropshipping, and global export services, with production bases in Guangzhou, Nanning, and Chongqing, China.
